Scope & method. A neutral data log: rows record what happened and the primary data (dates, territorial figures, votes, parties, recorded outcome), not interpretation. Where a figure is contested across sources (e.g. the Camp David II territorial percentages, the §181 split, the Peel transfer estimate), the competing figures are listed as data points rather than adjudicated. Reception, responses, and counter-offers are logged as their own dated events so the response is not buried in the proposal's outcome. Evidence tags mark whether each row rests on a held primary document P, a held secondary C, or general/established knowledge G pending corpus anchoring.

The analytical layer — strands, the symmetric refusal-ledger, the territorial-arithmetic and contested-offer audits, and the competing interpretive readings — is kept separate from this data, in the research repository (disposition-of-the-land-1915-2020.md), built on ≈38 held works including 12 primary-document anchors (the McMahon–Hussein, Churchill, Peel, Woodhead and 1939 White Paper command papers, the Mandate text, UNSCOP A/364, and the Israel-Arab Reader's verbatim 242 / 338 / Sinai II / Camp David / Fahd / Fez / Reagan texts).

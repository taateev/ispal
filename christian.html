Why this record is thin on coercion — a finding, not a gap

Set this page beside the Jewish one and the contrast jumps out: that page is a dense catalogue of violence, extortion, and expulsion; this one is mostly growth, patronage, and institutions. The near-absence of an extortion-and-massacre column here is structural — a result, not an omission.

Extraction took the form each community's vulnerability allowed. The shared weakness — being small and unprotected — gets attacked through whichever asset a community holds. The Old Yishuv was small, non-productive, and lived on a remittance pipeline (halukah) with no land and no early protector, so it was bled as cash extortion that compounded into a debt spiral. The Samaritans were tiny, of contested legal standing, and protector-less but did hold land — so they suffered land encroachment and near-extinction. The Christians were large, rooted on their own village land, of secure ahl al-kitāb status, and backed by great powers — so they escaped both: their share of extraction diffused into the ordinary peasant tax burden or was absorbed by the European-backed churches.

This is not "Christians had it easy." They bore the same dhimmi fiscal floor as the Jews (jizya, sumptuary law — see the 16th–18th-c. entry below), were caught in the 1834 revolt, and — as the visible proxies of European powers — drew a different kind of violence, the backlash (the 1840 anti-Christian wave, the 1856 Nablus riots, 1860). The Greek hierarchy, in turn, extracted its own Arab-Orthodox laity. The form of the pressure differed — set by asset-type × protection — not the fact of it.

Reading the record. Three registers are kept distinct: institutional/demographic growth (the metropole effect), the backlash it provoked (1840, 1860 — note the 1860 massacre was Damascus/Lebanon-centered, not Palestine), and the nationalist afterlife of the Arab-Orthodox laity. Anti-Zionism here is secular-nationalist; the European blood-libel repertoire (Damascus 1840) was Latin/French-channeled, with the Ottoman-Muslim state protecting the Jews — these are different things and not collapsed.

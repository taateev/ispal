03 · Synthesis

The land regime · 1831–1947

The land regime as a derivation graph — statutes, translations, commission reports, transactions and fatwas as a 53-node citation DAG. The Mandate never replaced Ottoman land law: its ordinances cite the 1858 Code verbatim, administered through a Turkish → French → English translation chain; and the commission cascade from Shaw to the 1940 Land Transfers Regulations mutates the "landless Arab" concept at every hop.

04 · Synthesis

Jewish defense organizations · 1907–1948

A formation ledger of nine Yishuv defense bodies (Bar-Giora to the Palmach) against their triggering violence. The thesis "nearly each was formed in response to Arab violence" tested and largely confirmed for the mainline (Haganah 1920, Irgun 1929, the Revolt-era forces) — with the early ones a fusion of defence and the conquest-of-guarding program, and Lehi the clean exception (formed against Britain).

05 · Synthesis

Jewish–Arab Labor · 1906–1948

How much Jews and Arabs actually worked together — cross-employment by the numbers, where the famous "still ~35% Arab" turns out to mean three different things (within Jewish agriculture vs. of wage earners vs. of the whole Jewish economy). The four streams of labor politics from Hebrew Labor to the binationalists, the joint-organizing reach-out that lost the internal fight by 1927, and the wage structure underneath it all — national census benchmarks plus a Haifa annual deep-dive.

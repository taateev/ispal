The 1947–48 war ran in two directions. This ledger is one of them — Arab attacks on the Yishuv: convoy and traffic ambushes, settlement assaults, the urban bombings, the road siege of Jewish Jerusalem, the ALA and Holy War Army offensives. Its counterpart, the depopulation ledger, records the other. 126 datable attacks, each tagged by type and perpetrator, with the retaliation or precipitant noted where the source states one. How to read this ledger

Seven attack types. Events are split by what the attack was: settlement assault (kibbutzim, moshavim, settlements), convoy & traffic ambush, urban bombing, sniping & shelling, siege / road interdiction, riot / mob, and assassination / other. The perpetrator chip on each row carries the force — local irregulars, the National Guard, the ALA (Qawuqji), the Holy War Army (Abd al-Qadir al-Husayni), a civilian mob, foreign volunteers, or a criminal band.

Two phases. The civil war (30 Nov 1947 – 14 May 1948) was the irregular/ALA/Holy-War offensive against the Yishuv. The 43 events after 15 May are the regular Arab armies' assaults on settlements during the conventional war — kept here because they are attacks on the Yishuv, but they overlap the Jewish-communities-overrun table on the depopulation ledger and are not civil-war irregular violence.

The accounting. The rows that state a toll sum to about 1,390 Jewish dead — indicative only. It mixes civilians and armed defenders, and a handful of large events (the Etzion bloc, Ben Yehuda St, the Hadassah convoy, Mishmar Ha'emek) carry most of it. It is consistent with the standard estimate of ~1,200–1,400 Jewish dead in the civil-war phase.

Symmetric rigor. 58 of 126 rows record a precipitant the source states — the opening 30 Nov ambush traces back through an LHI execution to a British raid on Jews; other attacks are framed as reprisal for Haganah or Irgun actions. The same discipline runs on the depopulation ledger: the two are the two directions of one war, not a scoreboard.

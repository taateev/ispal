Halukah & extraction in real terms — not obscure currency

The raw figures (kuruş, grush, piastres) are meaningless and don't form a reliable series. The corpus's one cost-of-living anchor makes them human-scale: ~150 kuruş/year supported a couple (100 a single man, 75 a single woman). As "couple-years of subsistence":

Charity → capital: the 1839 reversal changed what diaspora money could be. Pre-1839 it could only be subsistence charity (no legal land ownership; dhimmi disabilities). Post-1839 — Land Code 1858, Foreign Ownership 1867, consular protection — it became capital: the Rothschild Hospital (1854), Mishkenot Sha'ananim (1860), Mikveh Israel (1870), Petah Tikva (1878), "none of which could have existed under 1815 conditions." (No clean comparable totals exist; currencies differ; figures are estimates — the robust claim is the shape and the crisis-year magnitudes.)

Concurrent Natural Disasters

Not anti-Jewish violence, but their disproportionate Jewish toll reflects where subordination placed the community — the steep NW citadel slopes (the same ground in 1522 and 1837; the flatter-ground Muslim quarters survived comparatively), in poor, unmortared housing. A persecution-driven impoverishment effect registered seismically — not relocation (the quarter never moved) and not selective (Muslims died too, fewer). The 1758/59 + 1837 quakes and the 1812 plague together killed far more Jews than all human-perpetrated violence in the inventory combined.

Analytical Notes

Perpetrator disaggregation. Violence came from at least six distinct actor categories: Ottoman state officials (overwhelmingly extractive/fiscal); local Arab and Bedouin populations during power vacuums; Druze forces, alliance-conditional (1834 rescuers / 1838 plunderers); Egyptian military; Christian-instigated events (1840 Damascus blood libel); and Muslim creditors operating through the legal-enforcement mechanism.

Immigration as system input. Sephardi chain migration from 1492, Ashkenazi religious waves from 1700, and consular-regime-protected flows from 1838 produced the Old Yishuv that the harm events targeted. The halukah system funded its survival; extortion events are often the extraction side of halukah events.

The earthquake-violence interaction. The 1837 earthquake's ~2,000 Jewish dead (Safed + Tiberias) dwarfs all human-perpetrated casualties combined. The disproportion is structural, not selective: the community lived on the steep NW citadel slopes — the same ground in 1522 (Basola) as in 1837, so it is impoverishment-driven housing on static dhimmi-marginal ground, not relocation. A lagging structural effect of persecution, registered seismically; the natural-disaster "carve-out" is therefore not a clean category, but the base event still killed across communities (Muslims too).

Scope & provenance. The coercion catalog (violence / extortion / expulsion) runs 1516–1856; the thinning after 1856 is partly the inventory's construction, not only a measured decline — read the post-1839 drop against the population chart and comparison above, not as the table going silent. All pre-1839 population and money figures are estimates: no systematic count exists before the 1839 Montefiore censuses, and currency sums span a major debasement.

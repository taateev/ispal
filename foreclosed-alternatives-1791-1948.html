Israel's 1948 ethno-national state form was neither the inevitable execution of an original blueprint nor the innocent byproduct of self-defense. It was the surviving institutional form after a sequence of alternatives was progressively closed — by law, violence, demographic shock, imperial reversal, category-shift, and collapse of mutual credibility. This ledger dates each closing event, names its mechanism and the alternatives it bore on, and anchors it to the corpus. Foreclosure is not inevitability: the claim is about the narrowing of the field, never about the choices — and crimes — made inside it, which are marked separately on all sides. Companion pages: the defense-predicament ledger, the Mandate anti-Jewish-violence ledger, the 1948 depopulation ledger, and the land-regime page. What counts as foreclosure — and what doesn't

Four-part test · degrees of closure · six stages · symmetric guardrails

A foreclosed alternative must pass four tests: (1) it was a real alternative, not a retrospective fantasy; (2) it had an actual constituency or institution; (3) it had a plausible path to implementation; (4) a concrete mechanism closed it. Degrees are marked, not blurred: hard foreclosure (no actor could revive it by 1947 — the Holocaust-destroyed constituencies), severe narrowing (still pursued but past the survival threshold — dispersed emigration, territorialism, trusteeship), political marginalization (real advocates, never a majority — Ihud), and retrospective alternative (no enforcement vehicle existed). Arab-majority constitutionalism, its genuine democratic force notwithstanding, carries three separately dated closures: affirmatively foreclosed inside the Mandate frame in 1920–23, when constitutional participation was priced at accepting Balfour; credibility-foreclosed by the 1920–39 signal chain; and self-closed in May 1939, when the AHC rejected the White Paper's majority-state offer. Causal verbs are graded: foreclosed > severely weakened > accelerated the decline of — the ledger uses the weakest verb the evidence supports.

Temporal-register guardrail. The pre-war credibility chain (1920 → 1921 → 1929 → 1936–39) ran primarily in one direction — Arab-against-Jew — and closed the pre-war alternatives. Yishuv anti-civilian violence at scale came inside the 1948 war (Deir Yassin, Lydda, Hiram) and closed post-war alternatives: return, and trust in Israeli-majority guarantees — the symmetric entailment the framework owns rather than buries. Both registers use the same mechanism; they sit at different points in the causal chain and are tagged separately below.

Symmetric-rigor guardrails. The Zionist transfer current of 1937–39 is in the ledger, with its walk-backs. Yishuv-initiated violence is in the ledger, from Petah Tikva 1886 forward. The Jewish Quarter's protected surrender is in the ledger against the defeat-counterfactual's maximal reading. The Ottoman legal asymmetry beneath the "demographic majority" baseline is in the ledger. The Ottoman collapse is tracked as a double-sided closure: it ended Ottomanist Zionism and simultaneously stripped the Arab side of its strongest structural protection — the retention-interested sovereign whose demographic veto the Hamidian restrictions had been. Rhetoric and operational conduct are marked as separate axes; disputed translation chains (Azzam, Akhbar al-Yom) are flagged, not laundered.

How to read this ledger

Eight mechanism lanes. Baseline (the pre-Zionist subordination record — why every non-sovereign arrangement depended on an external guarantor), alternative live (a path is articulated or institutionalized — foundings, platforms, official positions), reclassification (racial recoding, sacralization, traitor-labeling: category-shifts that make compromise identities untenable), legal (states close paths by law), violence (physical destruction and credibility-by-atrocity), institutional (mediating structures fail), strategic (actors choose harder, narrower options), and demographic (population movement changes what is politically plausible). Most alternatives were closed by several mechanisms in sequence — the gain is in specifying which operated on which.

The "bears on" column tags each event with the tracked alternatives it opened, weakened, or closed, plus the graded causal verb. "Evidence" rows document the record without asserting closure — the baseline rows, the counterfactual rows, and the symmetric-rigor rows all use it.

Constraint ≠ choice ≠ crime. Foreclosure explains the narrowing field; it does not absorb agency. The 1948 operational decisions — Lydda-Ramle, Deir Yassin, Hiram, non-return, the invasion, the AHC's armed rejection — are choices within the narrowed field, and the atrocities among them are crimes, marked as such on every side.

Commission framings are not transparent fact. Shaw, Hope Simpson, Peel, Woodhead and the Survey are quoted as primary documents of what the British state found and did — their narrative choices are read against the corpus, per project discipline.

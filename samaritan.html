Samaritan Life under Ottoman Rule, 1516–1931

An indigenous Palestinian religious community ground to the edge of extinction — from ~1,400 in the 16th-century tax registers to a single endogamous remnant of ~140 at Nablus by 1900, its historic nadir, before a slow recovery.

Order-of-magnitude collapse over ~1.5 centuries (mostly the dissolution of the Gaza / Cairo / Damascus diaspora), then a long remnant plateau. The 1831–40 Egyptian decade (shaded) was the one relief window, in the community's own words; the 1839 Tanzimat line marks the reform era that, with no great-power patron, barely touched the Samaritans.
